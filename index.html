INSIGHTS

Trend Notes

01

Search is moving to AI answers

Brands now optimize to be cited in ChatGPT, Gemini and AI Overviews, not just ranked on Google.

02

Short video leads discovery

AI-assisted reels let small businesses publish daily at low cost.

03

Creative beats targeting

On Meta, testing creatives often moves results more than audience tweaks.

Campaign Lessons

A

Hindi creatives travel far

99.2% of 813,979 Instagram views came from non-followers, led by Reels.

B

One product per campaign

Product-wise Click-to-WhatsApp campaigns made it easy to spot the winner: ₹2.04 per conversation against ₹6.67 and ₹7.34.

C

WhatsApp is the real funnel

Ads, catalogue and Reels all end in one chat, so replies are tracked as conversations, not clicks.
